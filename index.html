Privacy Policy

Learn Korean: OwnWords  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Learn Korean: OwnWords ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Diary entries — the text you write (and speak) in the app, stored on your device to build your personal vocabulary.
  • Vocabulary & progress data — words saved, XP, streak, badges, and quiz results, all stored on your device.
  • Usage metadata — entry dates and counts, used for streaks and milestone tracking, stored on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not create user accounts or collect email addresses.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your diary entries to train AI models.
  • We do not store your data on our servers.

How we use your data

Your data is stored on your device only. When you translate a diary entry, the text is sent to our translation service (Claude AI by Anthropic) for processing and is not stored on our servers or by Anthropic beyond processing.

We do not share your data with advertisers or analytics platforms.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic Claude API — AI translation processing (anthropic.com/privacy)
  • Apple In-App Purchases — subscription billing (apple.com/legal/privacy)

Data retention

All your data is stored on your device. You can delete all data at any time from Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Your rights

  • Access: all your data is on your device and accessible within the app.
  • Deletion: delete all data from Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Correction: update or correct your data within the app.

For questions, contact us at learnyourownkorean@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or requests: learnyourownkorean@gmail.com
