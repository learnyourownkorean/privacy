Privacy Policy

Learn Korean: OwnWords  ·  Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Learn Korean: OwnWords ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Email address — used to create and manage your account.
  • Diary entries — the text you write (and speak) in the app, stored to build your personal vocabulary.
  • Vocabulary & progress data — words saved, XP, streak, badges, and quiz results.
  • Support messages — if you contact us via the in-app feedback form.
  • Usage metadata — entry dates and counts, used for streaks and milestone tracking.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your diary entries to train AI models.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features:

  • Diary entries are sent to our translation service (Claude AI by Anthropic) to generate Korean translations and vocabulary. They are not stored by Anthropic beyond processing.
  • All personal data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security — only you can access your own data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or analytics platforms.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI translation processing (anthropic.com/privacy)

Data retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.

Your rights

  • Access: request a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: request your account and all data be permanently deleted.
  • Correction: update or correct your data within the app.

To exercise these rights, contact us at learnyourownkorean@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or requests: learnyourownkorean@gmail.com
