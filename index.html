Privacy Policy

Learn Korean: OwnWords  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Learn Korean: OwnWords ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Your data stays on your device

Diary entries, vocabulary, progress (XP, streaks, badges), and quiz results are stored only on your device. We do not have access to this data. You can delete everything at any time from Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

What we send off-device

  • When you translate an entry, the text is sent to our translation provider for processing and is not stored on our servers. See anthropic.com/privacy for their data handling practices.
  • If you submit feedback, your message is sent to and stored on our server. Do not include personal information in feedback.
  • We store limited technical error logs to help fix bugs. These do not include diary text or personal content.
  • Your IP address may be temporarily processed for rate limiting. It is not stored or linked to your identity.
  • Subscription billing is handled by Apple. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do NOT collect

  • User accounts or email addresses
  • Location data
  • Advertising IDs
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your diary entries to train our own AI models

Permissions

The app may request microphone access for speech-to-text and notification permissions for study reminders. These are optional and can be managed in your device settings.

Your rights

  • Access: all your data is on your device and accessible within the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual entries or all data from Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling.

Questions? Contact us at learnyourownkorean@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
