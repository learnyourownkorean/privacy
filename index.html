Privacy Policy

Learn Korean: OwnWords  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Learn Korean: OwnWords ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Diary entries — the text you write (and speak) in the app, stored on your device to build your personal vocabulary.
  • Vocabulary & progress data — words saved, XP, streak, badges, and quiz results, all stored on your device.
  • Usage metadata — entry dates and counts, used for streaks and milestone tracking, stored on your device.
  • Feedback — if you submit feedback through the app, your message is sent to and stored on our server to help improve the app. Do not include personal information in feedback.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not create user accounts or collect email addresses.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect advertising IDs.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your diary entries to train AI models.
  • We do not store your journal content or vocabulary on our servers.

How we use your data

Your entries and progress are stored locally on your device. We do not run a user account system or store journal data in our own database. When you translate text, the content is sent to our translation provider for processing.

We do not share your data with advertisers or analytics platforms.

Technical data

We may store limited technical error logs to help us fix bugs. These logs do not include diary text or personal content. We may temporarily process a random device identifier and IP address for abuse prevention and rate limiting. These are not stored in error logs or linked to your identity.

Permissions

The app may request microphone access for speech-to-text diary entry and notification permissions for study reminders. These are optional and can be managed in your device settings.

Third-party services

  • Translation provider — diary text is sent to our translation provider for processing. See anthropic.com/privacy for their data handling practices.
  • Apple In-App Purchases — subscription billing (apple.com/legal/privacy)

Data retention

Your journal entries and progress are stored on your device only. You can delete all data at any time from Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. Technical error logs on our server are retained for diagnostic purposes and periodically purged.

Your rights

  • Access: all your data is on your device and accessible within the app.
  • Deletion: delete all data from Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Correction: update or correct your data within the app.

For questions, contact us at learnyourownkorean@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or requests: learnyourownkorean@gmail.com
